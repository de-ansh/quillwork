Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quillwork is self-hosted software. You run it on your own computer, and there is no Quillwork server, account or analytics. Its maintainer never receives your data.

What the app stores

Everything is stored in local files on your own computer: your settings (profile and schedule), your drafts and published posts, and your LinkedIn access token.

Who it talks to

  • LinkedIn: to sign you in (your name and member ID) and to publish the posts you approve on your own profile, using the scopes openid, profile and w_member_social.
  • Google Gemini API: your profile settings and recent posts are sent to write drafts. Requests use store=false. Google's own terms apply.

Quillwork does not read your feed, connections or messages, and it never posts anything you have not approved.

Removing your data

Delete the app folder (or posts.db, settings.json and token.json). You can revoke the app's access at any time in LinkedIn under Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services.
